# Rowan — the person this app used to be for

**Retired by [change 0004](../changes/0004-recipe-book.md).** No workflow names
this persona, and `@retired` is what says that is deliberate rather than a gap —
the same job [`../features/RETIRED.md`](../features/RETIRED.md) does for spent
rule ids. Without the tag, the persona→workflow gate would read this file as an
orphan and fail the build.

Versions 0001–0003 were built for Rowan, who kept a short list of things to do
today, wanted them out of their head in three seconds, and wanted them gone once
done. Notepads were their second sticky note.

Rowan is not [Nell](nell.md), and the difference is not a matter of degree:
Rowan's items existed to be got rid of, and Nell's exist to be kept. The
reasoning written for Rowan stays in
[`../changes/0001-core-todo-list.md`](../changes/0001-core-todo-list.md) through
[`0003`](../changes/0003-notepads.md) as the history of a product that changed
direction, and is **not evidence about what Nell wants**. That is the whole
reason this file still exists: a persona nobody deletes is a persona nobody
accidentally cites.

What carried over, and why, is in
[change 0004](../changes/0004-recipe-book.md): one browser tab, no backend,
nothing between the person and typing, and a list that never rearranges itself
under the cursor.
